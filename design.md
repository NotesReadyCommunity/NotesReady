# NotesReady — Design System & Visual Standards

## 1. Brand Identity: Concept 04
- **Emblem**: Custom geometric 'N' with smooth curves, continuous contours, and solid presence.
- **Wordmark**: "NotesReady" set in clean, high-precision neo-grotesque sans-serif.
- **Lockup Variations**: Horizontal (Emblem + Wordmark), Stacked, and Emblem Standalone.
- **Scalability**: Legible at all sizes from 16px favicon up to large hero brand displays.

## 2. Monochrome & Neutral Foundation
- **Policy**: Per official brief, brand color accents remain intentionally undecided until application visual hierarchy matures.
- **Theme Support**:
  - Light Mode: Pure white background `#FFFFFF`, subtle zinc/slate surfaces `#F8F9FA`, `#F1F3F5`, crisp black `#09090B` text.
  - Dark Mode: Deep neutral zinc `#0A0A0B` background, elevated surfaces `#121215`, `#18181B`, `#27272A`, crisp white `#F4F4F5` text.
- **Borders**: Subdued 1px borders (`rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` on light, `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` on dark).

## 3. Anti-AI Design Mandate
- No neon cyan/purple glowing blobs.
- No floating 3D meaningless geometric shapes.
- No fake SaaS dashboard mockups.
- No excessive glassmorphism.
- Spacing, typography, and contrast designed with human intentionality and restrained motion.
