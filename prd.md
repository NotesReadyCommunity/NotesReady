# NotesReady — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Vision & North Star
NotesReady is a modern digital workspace where individuals and teams can capture, organize, remember, connect, and collaborate on information.
- **North Star**: "Simple enough to start instantly. Powerful enough to grow with you."
- **Core UX**: "Invisible complexity."

## 2. Target Audience
General-purpose: Knowledge workers, researchers, software engineers, writers, professionals, students, creators, and collaborative project teams.

## 3. Core Product Modules
1. **Workspace & Navigation**: Multi-workspace support (Personal & Shared), collapsible sidebar, recents, favorites, trash, archive.
2. **Editor**: Modular headless ProseMirror/Tiptap engine supporting rich text, checklists, code blocks, callouts, tables, backlinks, and media.
3. **Organization**: Notebooks, nested folders/sections, tags, backlinks, and bidirectional note links.
4. **Search**: Instant global search, title and content matching, tags, filters, and keyboard-driven command palette (`Ctrl+K`).
5. **Real-Time Collaboration**: Multi-user editing, ephemeral presence/cursors, role-based sharing (Viewer, Editor, Owner), and threaded comments.
6. **Information Capture**: Fast typing, drag-and-drop, attachments, clipboard paste, and future web clipper.
7. **Security & Privacy**: Server-side authorization, unguessable cryptographic tokens, data encryption, export, and account deletion.
8. **Marketing Website (`notesready.in`)**: High-converting, product-led marketing site inspired by Evernote and Notion, showcasing real UI, clear CTAs, and transparent legal/trust pages.
